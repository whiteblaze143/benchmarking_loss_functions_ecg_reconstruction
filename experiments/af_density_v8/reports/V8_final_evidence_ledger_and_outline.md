## Part II: Measurement-Science Paper 1 Manuscript Outline

**Working Title:** *Measurement Reliability of Temporal Atrial Fibrillation Density Under Reference Annotation Ambiguity, Discretization, and High-Burden Conditioning*  
**Target Venue:** *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering* / *Circulation: Arrhythmia and Electrophysiology* / *npj Digital Medicine* (Measurement-Science Methodology & Validation Track)  
**Core Thesis:** Charitos AF density ($D$) suffers from three coupled structural failure modes—(1) sensitivity to sub-10s non-AF annotation semantics, (2) grid-discretization collapse and false-gap amplification by ambulatory ECG detectors, and (3) a $1/(1-B)$ mathematical singularity near unit burden—and should be co-reported with continuous AF aggregation ($G$), fractional AF occupancy, and partial-identification uncertainty bounds whenever episodic architecture matters.

### Planned Paper Architecture

1. **Section 1 — Introduction & Conceptual Problem** (~700 words)
   - Motivation: Clinical telemetry increasingly reduces multi-hour AF recordings to scalar burden $B$ or temporal clustering metrics such as Charitos AF density ($D = 2A/(1-B)$), Butkuvienė/Petrėnas AF aggregation ($G = 2A$), and episode continuity ($S_{\max}$).
   - Gap: Prior literature evaluated these estimators mostly in selected low-to-moderate burden or noise-free settings, ignoring denominator-near-zero conditioning, discrete grid failure, and reference-label ambiguity during ventricular overrides.
   - Contribution summary framed strictly around **Three Measurement Levels** (Reference Semantics $\to$ ECG Observability & Discretization $\to$ Mathematical Conditioning).

2. **Section 2 — Mathematical Conditioning Theory** (~1,200 words)
   - Formal definitions of $B$, $D$, $G = (1-B)D$, and $S_{\max}$.
   - **Theorem 1** (Residual-gap bridging amplification $\left|\Delta D/\Delta B\right| = 1/(1-B)$ vs exact $\Delta G = 0$ invariance and consolidation blindness).
   - **Theorem 2 & 3** (Equal-burden $L^1$ disagreement bounds $\delta / B$ and $\delta / [B(1-B)]$; localized non-trivial verification across `66/66` cases and `426/426` swaps; boundary continuity asymmetry at $B \to 1^-$ vs $B \to 0^+$).
   - **Theorem 4** (Uniform fractional-bin bounds $4h/T$ and $4h/[T(1-B)]$).

3. **Section 3 — Cohorts, Reconciled Timeline Semantics & Experimental Protocol** (~1,100 words)
   - Three-cohort multi-center sample inventory (`IRIDIA-AF`, `LTAFDB`, `SHDB-AF`: `379` files, `11,701.45 h` raw / `10,952.47 h` known; `n = 182` fixed `12h` evaluation panel + `n = 25×6h` deep-model panel).
   - Exact reference layers (`R0_Native`, `R1_Waveform_*`, `R2_VT_Unknown`, `R2_VT_SBR60_Unknown`, `R3_gap*`) with strict state nomenclature (`NATIVE_ALL_AF`, `ALL_KNOWN_AF`, `OPERATIONAL_ALL_AF`, `MIXED`).
   - Detector & discretization suite: fixed partial-bin-safe binary oracles (`10s..60m`), 4 fractional within-bin placement oracles (`LeftAligned, RightAligned, Centered, SplitEnds`), `RR_GBDT_AutoQRS`, `RR_GBDT_RefBeats`, and `ECGFounder` (`Raw` & `ZNorm`).

4. **Section 4 — Empirical Results** (~1,800 words)
   - **4.1 Level 1 Sensitivity & Partial Identification:** `12h` `1,078 < 10s` gap breakdown (`704 [65.31%]` native ventricular vs `710 [65.86%]` automated override, `353` `SBR`, `16` `N`); `12/19` and `16/19` shift to `ALL_KNOWN_AF` (`0/4` SHDB, `0/6` IRIDIA); `EXACT_EXHAUSTIVE_WHOLE_INTERVAL` (`29/38`) vs `SAMPLED_ATTAINABLE_EXTREMA_ONLY` (`9/38`) partial-ID intervals.
