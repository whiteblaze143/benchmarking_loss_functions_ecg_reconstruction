6.1 Why missing_leads_v2 is canonical

This evaluation scores reconstructed missing leads against the measured 12-lead ECG and prevents observed-lead passthrough from inflating performance. Rows from legacy_v1 are not pooled with it. The reference rows describe the measured-signal benchmark; they are not trained reconstruction models.

The database currently contains all 160 U-Net models, 37 MSVAE models, and no ECG-AIM models under missing_leads_v2. Therefore:

  • U-Net supports a complete within-architecture clinical factorial analysis.
  • MSVAE supports only explicitly labeled partial/descriptive analysis.
  • ECG-AIM has no clinical result in this database; it is missing evidence, not a negative outcome.
  • Cross-architecture clinical rankings are blocked by differential coverage.
