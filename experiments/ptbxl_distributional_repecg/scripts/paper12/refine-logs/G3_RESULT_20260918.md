# Paper 12 G3 Residual-Adequacy Result

**Verdict: FAIL. G4 remains blocked.** This verdict applies to the frozen 1,024-coordinate diagonal location-scale model with log-scale clamp `[-4,2]` and the preregistered 1,000-update budget.

## Decisive results

Across origins 0, 4, 8 and 12, patient-equal conditional NLL was `-3.748` to `-3.769`, while the matched phase-only baseline was `-4.503` to `-4.537`. Conditional-minus-phase-only differences were `+0.753` to `+0.768`; every patient-bootstrap 95% interval excluded zero in the failing direction.

Wrong-patient history changed NLL by only `0.0017` to `0.0145`. Label-matched wrong history was essentially indistinguishable and did not show a consistent direction across origins.

Location dependence decreased at every origin by `0.0064` to `0.0190`. Scale dependence decreased by only `0.00015` to `0.00073`; only origin 8 had a bootstrap interval excluding zero.

Standardized-innovation variance was `0.449` to `0.492`, not approximately one. Between 93.2% and 95.4% of evaluated log-scales hit the lower clamp and more than 99% were below `-3.8`. The scale head therefore did not learn an admissible real-data standardization.

The record-specific phase-permutation model had NLL `-3.7508` versus ordered-origin-0 `-3.7562`, a small change in the required direction that cannot rescue the failed phase-only and scale gates.

## Interpretation

The qualified Nyström representation does not validate the frozen diagonal conditional location-scale architecture. The lower clamp is materially incompatible with the coordinate magnitudes, but removing or changing it after observing G3 would define a new model version. Any rescue must be preregistered and rerun from G1/G2 as a new development protocol; these failed results remain immutable.
