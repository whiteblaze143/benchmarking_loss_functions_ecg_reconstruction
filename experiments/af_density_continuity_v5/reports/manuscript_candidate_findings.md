# Candidate Manuscript Findings: AF-DENSITY V5

1. **Measurement Identifiability (RQ1):** Coarsening to hourly bins produces median absolute density error of 0.1305 and leaves 95.2% of windows with severe continuity ambiguity (Delta S_max >= 0.25).
2. **Electrophysiological Heterogeneity (RQ2):** Patient-matched pairs with equivalent burden and density exhibit a mean difference of -17.05 percentage points in rapid AF fraction Q120.
3. **Temporal Decoupling (RQ3):** Rapid AF (r(t) > 120 bpm) does not strictly co-localize within the shortest 80% AF concentration interval (median Delta C = 2.1 pp).
