# Experiment Plan: SemiSegECG Delineation for Reconstructed ECGs

**Problem**: The current LUDB/RDB oracle evaluator measures voltage and morphology at fixed expert annotations but cannot estimate P/QRS/T boundary timing. NeuroKit was not reliable enough to serve as the independent delineator.

**Method thesis**: A content-pinned ViT-Tiny + Mean Teacher segmentation model can become a separate delineation instrument only if it first demonstrates acceptable, coverage-complete timing accuracy on untouched original ECGs; the same frozen instrument can then quantify paired timing degradation in reconstructed ECGs.

**Date**: 2026-08-22

## Evidence and frozen inputs

- Paper: Park et al., *SemiSegECG: A Multi-Dataset Benchmark for Semi-Supervised Semantic Segmentation in ECG Delineation*, CIKM 2025, DOI `10.1145/3746252.3760790`.
- Official source: `external/semiseg/semi-seg-ecg`, shallow-cloned from `https://github.com/bakqui/semi-seg-ecg` at commit `cf22fb55b7e85f01c4450deb085f8ce5b0932dcb`.
- Released checkpoint: `external/semiseg/semi-seg-ecg/vit_tiny-mean_teacher.pth`, SHA-256 `6a965b86314d3dcd6b1ba91833c84f8b63984e30191f0842d1784ef9cd9aeb07`, epoch 55, containing separate `model` and `model_ema` states.
- Downloaded preprocessing notebook: `external/semiseg/semi-seg-ecg/LUDB.ipynb`, SHA-256 `973f7aaa9afc9f689ba945c96d3e819965043ca93b47c76e6acfb04ddd37d11b`.
- Checkpoint architecture: single-lead input, 2,500 samples at 250 Hz, patch size 25, ViT-Tiny width 192/depth 12, FCN head with four classes: background=0, P=1, QRS=2, T=3.
- Checkpoint preprocessing: 10 seconds, 0.67–40 Hz fifth-order SOS filtering, per-example z-score normalization. Production inference must not use label-dependent padding or cropping.
- Compatibility evidence: the released checkpoint calls its backbone `vit_seg_tiny` and algorithm `mean_teacher_wo_cutmix`, names absent from the cloned release. A read-only smoke established that both checkpoint states strict-load into the released `vit_tiny` + `FCNHead`, yield finite `[1,4,2500]` logits, and predict all four classes on real LUDB record 1 lead I.

The paper does not show that Mean Teacher is universally best. ViT-Tiny + MT is the most defensible timing-oriented choice: it has the best LUDB in-domain ViT mIoU at all four label fractions, ties the lowest merged cross-domain average interval MAE at 14.9 ms, and has the best mECGDB average interval MAE at 20.7 ms. ST++ has the highest merged mIoU (84.7%), and other methods lead individual intervals; therefore the integration must retain comparator gates.

## Claim map

| Claim | Why it matters | Minimum convincing evidence | Linked blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1. Frozen ViT-Tiny + MT is a valid boundary-timing instrument on original ECGs. | Reconstruction timing errors are uninterpretable if the evaluator itself misses boundaries. | On the untouched official LUDB test subjects, report detection coverage and patient-clustered timing distributions for every onset/offset. P/QRS/T offset gates must be evaluated directly; no conversion from mIoU or interval MAE. | B1, B2 |
| C2. Frozen delineation reveals reconstruction-induced timing distortion independently of the oracle amplitude metrics. | This fills the missing evidence layer without replacing the existing oracle evaluator. | Paired original-versus-reconstructed changes with identical preprocessing, model state, postprocessing, beat matching, and records; stable ordering on LUDB and directionally consistent selected-model results on RDB. | B3, B5 |

**Anti-claims to rule out**:

- High mIoU alone implies clinically acceptable P/QRS/T offset timing.
- Gains arise from using LUDB training subjects, ground-truth-dependent padding, test-set postprocessing, or silently dropping failed beats.
- The EMA teacher is automatically better than the student, even though official inference loads `checkpoint['model']`.
- A learned evaluator can replace expert annotations or clinical adjudication.

## Paper storyline

- Main paper must prove: source-signal ceiling; explicit boundary timing and coverage; paired reconstruction-induced timing deltas; external/failure analysis.
- Appendix can support: official notebook parity, student-versus-EMA comparison, supervised ViT comparator, quantization/postprocessing sensitivity.
- Intentionally cut: retraining all five semi-supervised algorithms, tuning on LUDB test data, dense probability storage for every model, and any NeuroKit production dependency.

## Active upstream one-lead reconstruction baseline

The one-lead ECG-AIM screen is an upstream checkpoint-selection experiment for the reconstruction systems that will later enter B3/B5. Its frozen baseline is **A0: unconditioned ECG-AIM**, trained for 10 epochs with seed 42, one observed lead, and the same optimizer/data protocol as the spatial variants. This is a full training run, not a `--max_batches` smoke test.

Preliminary completed results for loss mask `1010010`, observed Lead I:

| Variant | Spatial conditioning | Validation loss | Best missing-lead Pearson | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| A0 baseline | None | 0.4620 | **0.7007** | Complete, one seed |
| B1 Panorama | Local grouped-angle encoder | 0.4642 | 0.6999 | Complete, one seed |
| C1 Panorama hybrid | Learned + grouped-angle encoder | **0.4614** | 0.6989 | Complete, one seed |

A0 is the **provisional Pearson leader** by `+0.0008` over B1 and `+0.0018` over C1. These differences are too small for a superiority claim from one seed. The queue must finish D1/E1, the author-faithful Panorama control, both Lead I/II inputs, and masks `1010010`/`1000000`. Additional seeds are deferred until this single-seed screen identifies which configurations merit confirmation. The author-faithful control imports Panorama's released `ThetaEncoder` and uses separate multiplicative source/query projections without the local `0.1` spatial gain.

The exact-Theta conditioning branch was initially specified as a conditional `2^3` factorial with Panorama's released `ThetaEncoder` fixed on:

| Cell | Learned lead-ID residual (L) | Relative source→target geometry (R) | Repeated decoder FiLM (F) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| T000 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| T001 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| T010 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| T011 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| T100 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| T101 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| T110 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| T111 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

The full 32-job expansion was cancelled before any exact-Theta job ran. Only T000 and T111 are retained on the canonical mask `1010010` + observed Lead I as implementation-equivalence checks. L, R, and F remain independently implemented with no fixed `0.1` gains in this branch, but the intermediate cells are not scientifically justified for production compute. A0 remains the external zero-geometry control. PA1 remains a separate mechanism control because it changes fusion to Panorama's multiplicative source/query operator.

**Interpretation caveat**: the grouped and exact theta encodings contain the same 12 scalar features in different orders. A learned dense projection can absorb that permutation, so an encoder-type difference does not establish greater representational capacity; it may reflect implementation fidelity, initialization, or optimization. T000/T111 are therefore implementation checks only; no L/R/F factorial-effect claim will be made from this reduced branch.

Two mechanism controls are retained across masks `1010010`/`1000000` × observed Lead I/II:

- **CM1 capacity-matched control**: replace anatomical angle features with deterministic fixed random codes matched to their global mean and variance, while retaining exactly the same trainable projection and parameter count as B1. This tests whether B1 benefits from anatomical geometry rather than extra pathway capacity.
- **PG1 permuted-geometry control**: cyclically derange the lead-to-geometry assignment while retaining the exact B1 architecture and parameter count. This tests whether correct anatomical correspondence matters.

F1 is not queued. The available `external/lead_field.md` is a paper capture, not an executable asset: it provides no pretrained operator, checkpoint, compatible geometry inputs, or validated ECG-AIM adapter contract.

## Protocol contract

### Data and splits

1. Recover and hash the official SemiSegECG subject-level indices. LUDB primary analysis is restricted to the official held-out test subjects; training and validation subjects cannot enter confirmatory timing estimates.
2. Keep the existing all-200-record oracle analysis as a separate amplitude/morphology result. Never merge it with the learned-delineator test estimate without an explicit cohort column.
3. Treat each lead independently, matching the checkpoint training contract. Primary reconstruction inference uses missing precordial leads V1/V3/V4/V5/V6; all 12 leads are secondary.
4. Use RDB only after its current oracle run and label mapping are complete. Preserve its diagnostic subgroup fields and distinguish lead-specific from consensus labels.

### Inference and preprocessing

1. Resample 500 Hz LUDB input to 250 Hz with a pinned Fourier/scipy implementation; filter at 0.67–40 Hz; z-score per single-lead 10-second record.
2. Do not reproduce `ecg_padding_by_label` from the downloaded notebook in production: it uses the first/last annotation to alter the signal and is unavailable on reconstructed or deployment ECGs. Reproduce it only in an appendix parity diagnostic.
3. Load student and EMA states as distinct `delineator_variant` identities. Select one using validation data only, then freeze it before test evaluation. The official student state is the default unless the prespecified validation comparison favors EMA.
4. Stream logits through postprocessing. Do not save full `[N,4,2500]` probabilities except for a small, stratified audit set. Save compact components, confidence summaries, and timing rows.
5. Record repository commit, checkpoint SHA, state name, preprocessing SHA, split SHA, dataset SHA, postprocessing SHA, and evaluator SHA in every evaluation identity.

### Boundary extraction and matching

- Convert argmax classes into connected P/QRS/T components. Apply only validation-selected deterministic rules: minimum duration, maximum short-gap merge, legal P→QRS→T order, and edge exclusion.
- Match predicted and reference waves one-to-one within prespecified beat windows anchored to QRS. Use an assignment procedure that prevents one prediction matching multiple references.
- Count unmatched references as false negatives and unmatched predictions as false positives. Never encode missing intervals as zero and include them in MAE.
- The official notebook is parity-only: it omits the last beat in multi-beat records and substitutes zero for missing intervals. The production evaluator must correct both behaviors.
- Primary timing coordinates are mapped back to the 500 Hz reference clock. Report a sensitivity analysis at the native 250 Hz/4 ms grid and with linearly interpolated logit crossings on the 500 Hz/2 ms grid; do not imply interpolation adds signal information.

### Metrics and clinical gates

For P/QRS/T onset and offset separately, report signed bias `mu`, sample SD `sigma`, MAE, median absolute error, p95 absolute error, sensitivity, positive predictive value, failure rate, and number of contributing patients/beats/leads. Also report PR, QRS, and QT interval signed error/SD/MAE and class-specific IoU/Dice excluding and including background.

Prespecified offset gates supplied for this study:

| Marker | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| P offset | `abs(mu) <= 10 ms` and `sigma <= 15 ms` |
| QRS offset | `abs(mu) <= 10 ms` and `sigma <= 11 ms` |
| T offset | `abs(mu) <= 15 ms` and `sigma <= 30 ms` |

A gate is not passed if coverage is below the validation-frozen minimum, the confidence interval is too wide to establish compliance, or failures were excluded from the denominator. Report patient-cluster bootstrap 95% intervals and per-record summaries; beats and leads from one patient are not independent replicates.

## Experiment blocks

### B1: Adapter and official-parity reproduction

- **Claim tested**: the released artifact can be reproduced and integrated without silent architecture or preprocessing drift.
- **Compared systems**: official notebook calculation; strict student adapter; strict EMA adapter.
- **Metrics**: checkpoint load coverage, finite outputs, class occupancy, official mIoU/interval MAE parity, runtime and peak memory.
- **Success criterion**: exact key load; deterministic output; documented explanation for any paper/notebook difference; no test-set tuning.
- **Failure interpretation**: source/checkpoint incompatibility or unavailable split provenance blocks production use.
- **Target**: appendix reproducibility table. **Priority: MUST-RUN.**

### B2: Original-signal source ceiling

- **Claim tested**: the delineator is accurate enough to measure reconstruction timing.
- **Dataset**: official held-out LUDB test subjects, original measured leads only.
- **Compared systems**: frozen selected MT state; released supervised ViT-Tiny checkpoint if acquired; optional historical NeuroKit numbers only as a labeled negative reference, never as a dependency.
- **Metrics**: full boundary/interval/coverage panel and the three offset gates above.
- **Success criterion**: all three offset gates pass with adequate coverage and patient-cluster uncertainty; failures remain in denominators.
- **Failure interpretation**: do not use the model to make absolute clinical timing claims. Proceed only as a paired sensitivity instrument if original-versus-reconstruction repeatability is demonstrably stable, clearly labeled exploratory.
- **Target**: main source-ceiling table. **Priority: MUST-RUN / HARD GATE.**

### B3: Reconstruction timing pilot

- **Claim tested**: timing changes respond to reconstruction damage rather than evaluator noise.
- **Systems**: original signal, identity round trip, controlled shifts/smoothing/amplitude perturbations, MSE mask `1000000`, current provisional balanced mask, and eventual Corr/Deriv candidate `1110000` when available.
- **Metrics**: paired delta in every timing/coverage endpoint, known-perturbation recovery, disagreement with oracle amplitude/morphology metrics.
- **Success criterion**: identity is near zero; injected shifts are recovered with correct sign/magnitude; model comparisons are stable under frozen postprocessing.
- **Failure interpretation**: evaluator is too insensitive or preprocessing/postprocessing dominates the signal.
- **Target**: main validation figure. **Priority: MUST-RUN.**

### B4: Necessity and simplicity checks

- **Claim tested**: Mean Teacher and the selected checkpoint state add value beyond architecture/postprocessing alone.
- **Ablations**: MT versus supervised ViT-Tiny; student versus EMA; no postprocessing versus minimal validation-frozen postprocessing; 250 Hz native versus 500 Hz logit interpolation.
- **Success criterion**: MT improves boundary timing/coverage, not merely background-inclusive mIoU; chosen postprocessing is small and robust.
- **Failure interpretation**: use the simpler supervised/student/native-grid system.
- **Target**: appendix ablation. **Priority: MUST-RUN for student/EMA; NICE-TO-HAVE for retrained comparator.**

### B5: Full selected-model and external evaluation

- **Claim tested**: learned timing conclusions survive model diversity and diagnostic/domain shift.
- **Dataset/task**: complete LUDB held-out reconstruction grid after a final catch-up; RDB diagnostic subgroups after the current RDB oracle run is complete.
- **Systems**: all LUDB masks for screening, then a prespecified compact Pareto set plus MSE and `1110000` on RDB. Do not run all 160 masks on RDB unless screening shows it changes inference.
- **Metrics**: paired timing panel, gate pass/fail, subgroup tails, source-signal ceiling per dataset, failure/abstention, rank agreement with oracle metrics.
- **Success criterion**: selected loss configurations do not improve waveform metrics by sacrificing boundary timing, and conclusions are not driven by one diagnostic subgroup.
- **Failure interpretation**: report endpoint discordance or domain-specific validity rather than a universal winner.
- **Target**: main comparison/subgroup tables. **Priority: MUST-RUN for compact Pareto set.**

| Milestone | Goal | Runs | Decision gate | Estimated cost | Principal risk |

| M1 | Reproduce and establish ceiling | Official parity plus student/EMA on original LUDB validation/test | Three offset gates and coverage | <=1 A100 GPU-hour after current jobs | Ground-truth-dependent preprocessing inflated reported performance |

| M4 | External/diagnostic confirmation | RDB source ceiling then 5–10 prespecified masks | Adequate ceiling and subgroup coverage | 4–15 GPU-hours | Domain/label-schema shift and 2,398-record scale |

No M1–M5 production run launches while `ecgaim_ludb_oracle_eval`, the current RDB oracle workflow, or ECG-AIM training is active. Production uses guarded detached tmux, an independent SQLite database, resume-by-content identity, and a disk-space gate.

## Compute and data budget

- **Must-run if the released checkpoint passes**: approximately 9–31 A100 GPU-hours, dominated by full reconstruction plus delineation rather than the ViT-Tiny forward pass. M1 must measure actual records/second and replace these ranges before M3.
- **Conditional retraining**: an additional 24–60 A100 GPU-hours for three 100-epoch seeds; do not authorize until a one-epoch throughput and learning smoke is reviewed.
- **CPU**: preprocessing, matching, SQLite aggregation, bootstrap analysis; cap workers so it does not overlap the current seven-core oracle workload.
- **Storage**: stream dense logits and retain compact event/interval rows. Target <5 GiB total; probability tensors are retained only for a small adjudication sample.
- **Data preparation**: official SemiSegECG split indices and label mapping must be downloaded, hashed, and reconciled with the existing LUDB/RDB identities.
- **Human evaluation**: manually inspect a frozen stratified set of successes, large errors, missing waves, pacing, AV block, extrasystoles, and bradycardia; this is error analysis, not clinical adjudication.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Training leakage**: restrict confirmatory LUDB results to official held-out subjects; never tune on test.
- **Ground-truth-dependent padding**: forbid it in production and quantify its effect only as an appendix parity ablation.
- **mIoU/timing mismatch**: make boundary timing and coverage the hard gate; report background-excluded class metrics.
- **Dropped failures**: maintain full denominators and explicit unmatched/missing/spurious rows.
- **Student/teacher ambiguity**: preserve both hashes/state names; choose on validation only.
- **Quantization**: report native-grid and interpolated-logit sensitivity.
- **Delineator-induced ranking**: compare original ceiling, controlled perturbations, and a compact second delineator/baseline where feasible.
- **Disk bloat**: never persist full-grid dense probabilities; transactionally write compact SQLite rows and atomic summaries.
- **Resume corruption**: skip only rows matching reconstruction checkpoint, delineator checkpoint/state, protocol, dataset, and split hashes.
- **License/provenance**: retain Apache-2.0 notices and pin the vendor commit; keep project adapter modifications separate and clearly attributed.
