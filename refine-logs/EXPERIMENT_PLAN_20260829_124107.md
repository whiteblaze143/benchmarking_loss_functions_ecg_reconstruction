# Experiment Plan: Evidence-Tier Reconciliation and Current ECG-AIM Program

**Problem**: The book mixes terminal screens, completed extensions, partial architecture grids, active queues, and external evaluations, causing current evidence to be omitted or pooled under incompatible contracts.
**Method Thesis**: ECG-AIM claims are defensible only when input contract, generation, training budget, seed, checkpoint selector, data/content fingerprint, and evaluation stage are held explicit; completed screening, convergence, and external validation must therefore be reported as separate evidence tiers.
**Date**: 2026-08-29

## Claim Map

| Claim | Why It Matters | Minimum Convincing Evidence | Linked Blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 primary | Wavelet/SSL or spatial conditioning improves one-lead ECG-AIM beyond matched A0, rather than winning through search or added capacity. | Lead-stratified A0→wavelet→SSL contrasts, capacity/permutation controls, at least three seeds, longer convergence, paired held-out endpoints, and external six-boundary analysis. | B1–B4 |
| C2 supporting | The physiological UEG second view contributes specifically to repolarization localization. | Morlet magnitude + Morlet phase versus Morlet magnitude + UEG phase at matched architecture/compute, with gains concentrated on valid T-on/T-off endpoints and reproduced externally. | B2–B4 |
| Anti-claim | A single seed, short budget, incompatible checkpoint generation, or validation-selected maximum is sufficient evidence. | Terminal failure accounting, generation-specific tables, current-contract compatibility gates, multiplicity control, and explicit negative/neutral outcomes. | B1–B5 |

## Paper Storyline

- Main paper must prove the matched one-lead mechanism chain and externally test the promoted model without development/test reuse.
- Three-lead seven-mask results remain a separate architecture/objective study; one-lead results cannot fill its missing cells.
- Completed results are frozen into generation-specific chapters. Active and pending queues appear only in the observatory.
- Preflights, archives, sentinels, and failed generations are engineering evidence, not pooled scientific rows.
- Cross-architecture claims are blocked when source/state-schema/content compatibility differs, even if checkpoint storage is remotely verified.

## Experiment Blocks

### B1: Completed one-lead screening and spatial evidence
- Claim tested: which configurations merit promotion under fixed seed and short budget?
- Data/task: PTB-XL, one observed lead (I or II), missing-lead reconstruction; `1110000` screen and legacy spatial grid.
- Systems: 120-cell wavelet/SSL screen; ten spatial variants × three masks × two leads.
- Metrics: reconstruction loss, Pearson mean/p05, P/QRS/T IoU, macro F1, mean IoU, boundary smoke metrics, training components, gradient norm, memory; spatial log validation loss/Pearson.
- Evidence: 115/120 screen cells complete with five named terminal failures; 60 spatial runs complete and 24 cancelled queue entries.
- Success criterion: promotion only through registered matched contrasts, never the global maximum.
- Failure interpretation: a neutral or lead-specific result limits the claim; it is not rescued by pooling leads.
- Destination: completed screening and completed spatial chapters.
- Priority: MUST-RUN analysis; training complete.

### B2: Promoted convergence generation
- Claim tested: do screen-level rankings survive 10/15 epochs?
- Systems: A0 raw, wavelet/no-SSL, Morlet-phase SSL, UEG-Wyatt SSL, real-UEG and promoted controls.
- Metrics: every stored validation/delineation/training/resource field; checkpoint composite and epoch-selection mismatch.
- Evidence: 23 historical ten-epoch summaries; eight completed fifteen-epoch summaries at audit; one running and thirteen pending in the current queue.
- Success criterion: stable matched advantage across leads and seeds, with no material reconstruction regression.
- Failure interpretation: convergence-order reversal invalidates screen-based promotion.
- Destination: convergence results chapter plus status-only observatory.
- Priority: MUST-RUN.

### B3: One-lead external RDB evaluation
- Claim tested: do completed spatial checkpoints preserve waveform fidelity and six genuine onset/offset boundaries on RDB?
- Data/task: full-stage `onelead_rdb_semiseg_screened_v1`; P/QRS/T onset and offset only, no invented peaks.
- Metrics: F1/PPV/sensitivity at 20 ms; timing bias/MAE/p95; region sensitivity/specificity/PPV/Dice/IoU; Pearson mean/p05; MSE mean/p95; bias and limits of agreement.
- Evidence: 122 evaluation rows representing pilot/full stages, 62 full-stage IDs including two sentinels; 2,304 boundary, 1,152 region, 384 signal rows, and 60 decisions.
- Success criterion: promoted trained checkpoints outperform prespecified baselines on both waveform tail and boundary endpoints.
- Failure interpretation: passing a screen is not external validation; endpoint disagreement must remain visible.
- Destination: one-lead RDB completed-results chapter.
- Priority: MUST-RUN analysis; evaluator complete.

### B4: Replication and blinded promotion
- Claim tested: are the A0→wavelet→SSL and Morlet→UEG effects reproducible rather than seed/search artifacts?
- Runs: three seeds for only prespecified survivors, repaired-mask `1111002`, then blinded LUDB/RDB once without threshold retuning.
- Metrics: paired per-record reconstruction, six-boundary timing, region overlap, tail behavior, calibration, bootstrap confidence intervals, and multiplicity-adjusted tests.
- Success criterion: directionally consistent effects with uncertainty excluding the practical-equivalence margin.
- Failure interpretation: downgrade to exploratory representation engineering.
- Priority: MUST-RUN before a physiological mechanism claim.

### B5: Three-lead parity and compatibility repair
- Claim tested: loss effects and ECG-AIM behavior under the locked I+II+V2 input contract.
- Current evidence: U-Net 160/160, MSVAE 155/160, ECG-AIM 123/160 checkpoints; `missing_leads_v2` clinical coverage 160/37/0; LUDB blinded 123 ECG-AIM + original; RDB oracle/blinded 31 promoted ECG-AIM models; 11 representation jobs complete.
- Compatibility gate: current audit marks 310 U-Net rows compatible and the 278 archived MSVAE/ECG-AIM rows incompatible with the current pinned evaluation contract. Remote verification proves recoverability, not comparability.
- Success criterion: retrain or formally freeze a generation-matched subset before cross-architecture inference.
- Priority: MUST-RUN for cross-architecture claims; otherwise report descriptive within-generation results only.

## Compute and Data Budget

- Current GPU work: one serial 15-epoch convergence queue; do not infer capacity from allocated memory alone.
- CPU evaluation: keep normalized results in compact SQLite; do not export per-record CSV copies.
- Storage: checkpoints remain content-addressed/remote-verified; databases store metrics and provenance, not checkpoint tensors.
- Biggest bottleneck: compatible generation parity and seed replication, not another broad hyperparameter sweep.

## Risks and Mitigations

- Single-seed search bias: promote only registered contrasts and replicate survivors.
- Contract incompatibility: gate comparisons on data, state-schema, selector, and content fingerprints.
- Stage duplication: distinguish pilot/full and 3/10/15-epoch generations.
- Development/test reuse: freeze thresholds and use a one-time blinded confirmation.
- Missing peaks in RDB: report only six valid onset/offset boundaries.
- Hardware instability: retain ECC deltas and artifact digest validation for every promoted run.
