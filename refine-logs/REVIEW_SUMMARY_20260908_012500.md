# Review Summary: Occam's Razor Refinement for Lean-CardioAIM

**Date**: 2026-09-08  
**Status**: ACTIVE / REVISED TO LEAN ARCHITECTURE  
**Review Focus**: Elimination of Overparameterization and Loss Interference

---

## 1. Summary of Critical Decisions

1. **Rejection of 8-Layer / 768-Dim Encoder**:
   - The original massive backbone was over-parameterized for 1-lead to 11-lead transfer.
   - The 4-layer / 512-dim configuration (`C1_enc4` + `C3_width512`) achieves $+0.005$ higher correlation on $N=2,183$ validation records while running 35% faster.
2. **Rejection of Whole-Lead Dropout**:
   - Whole-lead dropout disrupted the model's capacity to build deterministic projection mappings from Lead I to orthogonal leads.
   - Removing it (`K1_nold`) generated the largest single gain in the entire ablation suite ($+0.0105$ correlation, $+0.0179$ tail $p_{05}$).
3. **Rejection of Complex MMD / VCG Factorial Loss Regularization**:
   - Testing `L0_lean_best` with factorial mask `1111002` (activating VCG and MMD kernel 2) resulted in a sharp $-0.0130$ drop in correlation due to gradient competition between distribution matching and morphological fidelity.
   - Restoring the parsimonious triplet loss (`1110000`: MSE + Pearson + Derivative) establishes the clean, non-conflicted baseline.
4. **Preservation of Multi-Task Delineation Heads**:
   - Removing delineation heads (`K3_nodel`) caused a catastrophic $-0.0195$ collapse. Multi-task electrophysiological segmentation remains a non-negotiable core pillar.
5. **Rejection of Hard Deterministic Physical Graph Constraints**:
   - Hard-coding Einthoven's law (`B1` - `B4` cells) restricted non-linear volume conductor flexibility and degraded performance by $-0.004$ to $-0.046$. Soft inductive losses remain strictly superior.

## 2. Reviewer Gates Addressed

- **Gate A (Simplicity Audit)**: Does the paper propose unnecessary bells and whistles?
  - *Addressed*: All extraneous components (flow matching, MMD kernels, hard graph constraints, 8-layer depth) have been pruned.
- **Gate B (Statistical Rigor)**: Are improvements statistically significant across records?
  - *Addressed*: All decisions are validated using paired 10,000-sample bootstrap testing on $N=2,183$ PTB-XL records, reporting exact 95% CIs and $p_{\text{inferior}}$ values.
- **Gate C (Tail Robustness)**: Does the model fail on difficult recordings?
  - *Addressed*: The 5th percentile correlation ($p_{05}$) is tracked as a primary gate metric to prevent tail failure.
