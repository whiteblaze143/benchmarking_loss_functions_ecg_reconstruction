# Research Proposal: Paper 10 — Paired Acquisition-Intervention Stability

## Problem Anchor

Acquisition artifacts can create diagnostic shortcuts, but hospital identity is not itself a valid acquisition intervention. Paper 10 tests whether diagnostic states remain stable when a known, label-preserving acquisition transform is applied to the same raw ECG.

## Method Thesis

Given raw record `x_i`, a controlled acquisition transform `T_e`, and a frozen phase-KME map, learn a diagnostic state `Z_S` that is stable between `x_i` and `T_e(x_i)` while retaining diagnosis, and an auxiliary state `Z_A` that still identifies `e`.

## Exact Scope

The initial environment bank is `clean`, gain `0.8`, gain `1.2`, `500->250->500` Hz resampling, 20 dB noise, and 10 dB noise. These are executed before filtering, scaling, peak detection, phase normalization, and the frozen representation map. It does not include lead subsets, hospital/site membership, or unimplemented bandwidth transformations.

## Architecture and Objective

The shared existing backbone emits `Z_S,Z_A`. Diagnosis uses `Z_S` only and acquisition identity uses `Z_A` only:

`L_full = L_BCE(Y,h(Z_S(x_i))) + lambda_A L_CE(e,a(Z_A(T_e x_i))) + lambda_pair mean d(normalize(Z_S(x_i)),normalize(Z_S(T_e x_i))).`

The paired term is the paper’s intervention-specific mechanism. It is not described as mutual-information minimization or causal-feature identification. All weights and selection rules are frozen on fold 7 before fold 8 is read.

## Comparator Contract

- **ERM:** diagnosis BCE only, matched encoder and training budget.
- **IRMv1:** true per-environment gradient penalty, only after it receives balanced environment batches and an objective-specific unit test.
- **CORAL:** true `Z_S` mean/covariance alignment across environments, only after its loss executes and is tested.
- **Full paired model:** the objective above.
- **Removed:** `causirl`; there is no defined or implemented CausIRL objective.

## Must-Prove Claims

1. Paired stability improves worst-environment behavior over ERM for the prespecified transform bank.
2. The effect depends on same-record pairing, rather than a generic alignment penalty or collapse.

## Failure Conditions

The paper stops if transforms alter labels/identity, no valid paired views remain, `Z_S` collapses, the matched-pair destroyer is equally effective, or ERM matches/exceeds full on the frozen robust endpoint. A negative result refutes this architecture; it is retained and does not justify a hospital-causality claim.
