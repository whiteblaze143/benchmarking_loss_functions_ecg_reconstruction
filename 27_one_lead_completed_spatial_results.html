5.1 Experiment identity

This completed legacy study is distinct from the newer 48-cell spatial_arch_1lead_v1 queue. It crossed ten spatial/conditioning variants, three factorial masks (1000000, 1010010, and 1110000), and two observed leads, yielding 60 completed training runs. Twenty-four cancelled queue entries are retained in the queue ledger but are not model results.

The supported mechanism claim is therefore narrower: correct spatial metadata can matter conditional on the architecture, but its average advantage over A0 is small and configuration dependent.

## Geometry-versus-capacity falsification tests

```{python}
#| label: spatial-falsification-controls
matrix = completed.pivot_table(
    index=["factorial_mask", "observed_lead"],
    columns="variant", values=PRIMARY_METRIC)
contrasts = pd.DataFrame(index=matrix.index)
for label, candidate, reference in [
    ("E1 − A0", "e1_panorama_film", "a0"),
    ("E1 − permuted geometry", "e1_panorama_film", "pg1_permuted_geometry"),
    ("E1 − capacity matched", "e1_panorama_film", "cm1_capacity_matched"),
    ("B1 panorama − A0", "b1_panorama", "a0"),
    ("PA1 author fusion − A0", "pa1_panorama_author", "a0"),
]:
    contrasts[label] = matrix[candidate] - matrix[reference]
display(matrix.reset_index())
display(contrasts.reset_index())

long_contrasts = contrasts.reset_index().melt(
    id_vars=["factorial_mask", "observed_lead"],
    var_name="contrast", value_name="Pearson difference")
fig = px.bar(long_contrasts, x="contrast", y="Pearson difference",
             color="observed_lead", facet_row="factorial_mask", barmode="group",
             title="Matched spatial-control contrasts")
fig.add_hline(y=0, line_dash="dash")
fig.show()
```

True E1 geometry beats permuted geometry in all six mask×lead cells by
0.0030–0.0120 Pearson and beats the capacity-matched control by 0.0024–0.0096.
The consistency of direction is evidence that the model uses the geometry
labels rather than benefiting only from parameter count.

That result does **not** establish E1 as a generally improved base. Relative to
A0, E1 gains 0.0214 only for mask `1000000`, lead I; the other five differences
range from −0.0019 to +0.0033 and are practical ties at this precision. Likewise,
simple panorama and author-fusion effects change sign across masks and leads.
The supported mechanism claim is therefore narrower: correct spatial metadata
can matter conditional on the architecture, but its average advantage over A0
is small and configuration dependent.

Lead II is usually easier than lead I in this internal validation study, but

## Claim ledger

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Correct geometry is used by E1 | Supported descriptively | E1 exceeds permuted geometry in all six matched cells. |
| Gains are only extra capacity | Disfavoured | E1 exceeds the capacity-matched control in all six cells. |
| E1 is the improved base architecture | Not supported | Five of six E1−A0 differences are ties near zero; one lead/mask cell drives the visible gain. |
| Lead II is universally the best input | Not established | Internal averages favour II, but endpoint and external-cohort transport remain separate questions. |
| Spatial conditioning improves clinical delineation | Requires external evidence | These logs contain reconstruction validation metrics only. |
