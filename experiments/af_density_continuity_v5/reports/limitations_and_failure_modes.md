# Methodological Limitations and Failure Modes: AF-DENSITY V5

1. **Observational Retrospective Design:** The association between episode continuity and ventricular rate reflects concurrent electrophysiology, not a randomized intervention.
2. **Rate Estimation Window:** Ventricular rate is estimated over nonoverlapping 30-second windows; episodes shorter than 30s are excluded from rate calculations.
3. **Holter Duration Horizon:** The primary horizon is 12 hours of monitored ECG; long-term multimonth CIED dynamics may exhibit different multi-day aggregation properties.
4. **Medication Data:** Comprehensive medication history is available in SHDB-AF, but limited in LTAFDB and IRIDIA-AF.
