# Geometry-specific latent completion: preregistered experiment plan

Created: 2026-09-10 17:30:54 UTC

## Decision and claims

The 113-entry SAP benchmark changes the comparator: `lean2_T_patch10` is the frozen direct-regression frontier (test SAP mean patient correlation 0.83193; p05 0.62111), while the fixed constrained-VCG oracle reaches approximately 0.87587/0.74492. That gap is promising but does not show that VCG geometry is special. Earlier true/permuted/random geometry controls were similar, so neural training is forbidden until a validation-only specificity gate separates anatomical geometry from generic rank-2 completion.

At most two claims are in scope:

1. Observation-constrained rank-2 completion improves reconstruction over a matched direct seven-output decoder.
2. Fixed VCG geometry provides benefit beyond a generic training-derived rank-2 residual subspace.

Anti-claim: an apparent VCG gain is only low-rank regularization, reduced output dimension, or selection on the held-out test set.

## Frozen protocol

- Data: PTB-XL folds 1–8 for fitting `c_PCA` and `B_PCA`; fold 9 only for all model selection, oracle comparison, and neural gates. Fold 10 and EchoNext waveform evaluation are prohibited in this phase.
- Observed signal: Lead I. Independent missing targets: `[II,V1,V2,V3,V4,V5,V6]`. Leads III/aVR/aVL/aVF are deterministically derived from I and II and are never independent training targets.
- Primary metrics: patient-weighted `mean_independent_missing_r` and `p05_independent_missing_r`. Secondary metrics: independent-target MSE, per-lead R², SemiSeg P/QRS/T IoU and mIoU, QRS-duration MAE, Sokolow–Lyon MAE, and precordial variance retention.
- Trunk: `lean2_T_patch10_s42_l0`, selected in the pre-SAP fold-9 tracker. Frozen checkpoint SHA-256: `25fef94eda49c5ce853f226fba08e95824a09123dbcad3d12f0e2499010fc602`. Its config SHA-256 is `78b5485e43d848b34fa3019778be6f35e2a36359c5e7d0554d5964f77d57de87`.
- Random-subspace control: 100 independent Haar-distributed 7×2 orthonormal bases, NumPy seed 20260910. This seed and count are frozen before results are produced.
- Statistical test: 10,000 patient-level paired bootstrap replicates, seed 20260910. Material superiority requires delta mean independent correlation >= 0.005 and a 95% paired-bootstrap lower bound > 0.
- Compute: existing `.venv`, local NVIDIA A100, CUDA required for neural/SemiSeg inference. No environment rebuild.

## Experiment blocks

### B0 — integrity and data audit (must run)

Audit actual fold-9 limb identities for III, aVR, aVL, and aVF. Report max absolute error, RMSE, and per-lead values. A source-algebra max error above 0.002 waveform units (2 µV for these mV tensors) stops neural work pending investigation. Record exact split counts, patient counts, paths, hashes, software versions, and the frozen trunk-selection evidence. Inspect EchoNext metadata and counts only; do not access labels or evaluate waveforms. If a deterministic patient-disjoint pristine remainder can be defined from metadata, freeze its IDs/hash for a future phase; otherwise record that no defensible pristine holdout can be established.

Engineering amendment, 2026-09-10 17:42 UTC: the initial two-batch smoke observed a maximum 0.00150007 mV source-algebra residual, exactly at the half-step combinations expected when independently stored leads are quantized to 0.001 mV. The provisional 0.0001 mV threshold was below the source resolution and therefore invalid. It was replaced before the full oracle run by the 0.002 mV ceiling above. No full-cohort geometry result was inspected, and all scientific decision thresholds remain frozen.

### B1 — geometry-specificity oracle (must run before training)

Fit `c_PCA` from folds 1–8 by least squares in `Y = cI + residual`, where Y contains the seven independent missing leads. Fit the rank-2 orthonormal basis `B_PCA` by the top two eigenvectors of the uncentered training residual second moment; uncentered SVD is preregistered because inference is exactly `cI + Bz` with no intercept. Canonicalize eigenvector signs for reproducibility.

On fold 9 compare:

- G0: fixed constrained VCG oracle.
- P0: training-derived rank-2 residual PCA oracle.
- R0: 100 frozen random rank-2 residual bases, scored on the primary metrics and MSE.
- T0: frozen `T_patch10` direct frontier, as context only; it does not fit or select geometry.

Classify before neural training:

- `VCG_SUPPORTED`: G0 is within 0.005 of P0 on mean independent correlation and has comparable clinical metrics.
- `LOW_RANK_ONLY`: P0 exceeds G0 by more than 0.005 without compensating VCG clinical benefit.
- `FAIL`: neither oracle leaves useful headroom over T0 (less than 0.005 mean-independent-correlation improvement with positive paired-bootstrap lower bound).

The clinical-compensation rule is frozen as: VCG may compensate only if it wins at least two of {SemiSeg mIoU, QRS MAE, Sokolow–Lyon MAE, variance-retention log error}, loses none by more than {0.005, 1 ms, 0.02 mV, 0.05 log units}, and its mean-correlation deficit is at most 0.015.

### B2 — matched neural seed-42 comparison (must run only if B1 permits)

Train exactly three matched systems for 15 epochs, seed 42, with the frozen `T_patch10` trunk, data order, optimizer, augmentation, normalization, clinical auxiliary objective, parameter budget, and checkpoint rule (`r_ind,val`) held fixed:

- B1: direct seven-output decoder.
- M1: two-output decoder mapped through the fixed VCG residual geometry.
- C1: two-output decoder mapped through frozen `B_PCA` and `c_PCA`.

Loss is evaluated only on the seven independent missing targets. Derived limb leads are constructed only for complete-ECG clinical evaluation. No learned geometry, extra loss sweep, or test-set access is permitted.

### B3 — seed-42 inference and paired analysis (must run)

Evaluate B1/M1/C1 on fold 9 and report the complete metric panel, oracle gaps, failure strata, and 10,000-replicate patient-paired bootstrap intervals for M1−B1, C1−B1, and M1−C1. Claim 1 requires either latent model to meet the material-superiority rule against B1. Claim 2 requires a favorable M1−C1 paired interval; merely tying C1 is not evidence that VCG is special.

### B4 — replication and external holdout (conditional)

Seeds 43 and 44 are allowed only after a seed-42 material-superiority gate. PTB fold 10 and any defensible pristine EchoNext subset remain sealed until architecture, metric, and seed decisions are frozen. They are nice-to-have for a later confirmatory phase, not current-phase outputs.

## Baselines, stop rules, and exclusions

Baseline families are limited to direct regression (B1/T0), fixed VCG latent completion (G0/M1), and generic rank-2 completion (P0/C1/R0). Stop immediately for split leakage, non-finite metrics, observed-Lead-I mismatch, limb-algebra audit failure, missing provenance, or an oracle `FAIL`. If the result is `LOW_RANK_ONLY`, train B1 and C1 only for the low-rank claim; M1 may be retained as a negative control but cannot motivate a VCG claim. If `VCG_SUPPORTED`, train all three.

Out of scope: flow/diffusion models, metadata conditioning, AF/PPG branches, foundation-model conditioning, patient-specific geometry, learned nonlinear projection, new SSL, new loss sweeps, and retrospective changes to gates or seeds.
