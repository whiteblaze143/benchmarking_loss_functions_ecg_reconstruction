# Experiment Plan: Paper 10 — Paired Acquisition-Intervention Stability

**Problem:** acquisition-shortcut robustness under known, label-preserving ECG transforms.

**Method thesis:** same-record paired stability is the primary invariance signal; site identity is not used as an intervention.

## Claim Map

| Claim | Minimum convincing evidence | Block |
|---|---|---|
| C1: `Z_S` is stable to specified acquisition transforms without losing diagnosis | full beats ERM on patient-equal paired stability and worst-environment AUROC | B3 |
| C2: the effect requires correct pairing, not collapse or generic alignment | full beats mismatched-pair destroyer; `Z_A` predicts environment and `Z_S` does not | B2, B4 |

## Experiment Blocks

### B0: Intervention-artifact integrity — MUST-RUN

- Build all views from raw physical waveforms before the frozen preprocessing map.
- Require exact `(patient_id, ecg_id, label)` equality across views, deterministic transform seeds, valid-cycle accounting, and patient-disjoint folds.
- Reject the artifact if a transform changes labels, causes unreported eligibility attrition, or leaks fold 7/8 into the fit.

### B1: Synthetic identifiability gate — MUST-RUN

- Generate `X=S+U_e`, where labels depend only on `S` and `e` is randomized independently of `S,Y`.
- Verify the full loss reduces same-`S` state drift, preserves label prediction, keeps `Z_A` environment-predictive, and fails under different-`S` pairing.
- Also generate an impossible world where `Y` depends on `e`; the method must not be claimed to remove that dependence without task loss.

### B2: Objective-execution and pairing controls — MUST-RUN

- Unit-test that ERM, IRMv1, CORAL, and full compute distinct, finite nonzero objective terms from environment IDs.
- Test joint pair-order invariance, transformed-view paired stability, and a label-matched different-patient pairing destroyer.
- Remove any named variant without an executable, tested objective.

### B3: Frozen paired robustness development result — MUST-RUN only after B0-B2

- Train on folds 1-6; select `lambda_A`, `lambda_pair`, and optimizer settings on fold 7; do not read fold 8.
- Compare capacity-matched ERM, IRMv1, CORAL, and full over three seeds.
- Primary endpoint: patient-equal paired stability improvement and lower-bound robust task criterion in the project claim-gate matrix.
- Secondary: worst-environment macro AUROC, macro-AUPRC, calibration, per-transform AUROC change, and valid-view denominator.

### B4: Collapse and boundary analysis — MUST-RUN

- Fit held-out environment probes: `Z_A -> e` should be above chance; `Z_S -> e` should be near chance only because `e` is randomly assigned.
- Report `Z_S` variance/effective rank and diagnosis performance; a constant state fails.
- Evaluate excluded transforms only as an appendix boundary test. Lead subsets remain Paper 9 measurement interventions, not Paper 10 acquisition environments.

## Run Order

| Milestone | Gate | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | B0 artifact contract | Stop if provenance or eligibility fails |
| M1 | B1+B2 synthetic/objective tests | Stop if objectives are equivalent, pairing is unused, or collapse passes |
| M2 | B3 development grid | Advance only under frozen claim-gate matrix |
| M3 | B4 safeguards | Qualify claim scope; do not widen it |
