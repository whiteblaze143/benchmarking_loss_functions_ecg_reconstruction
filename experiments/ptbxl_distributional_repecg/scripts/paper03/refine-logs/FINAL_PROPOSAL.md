# Final Proposal: Paper 03

## Problem Anchor
Path signatures for sequential ECG modeling

## Core Thesis
Iterated integrals of paths compactly encode geometric shapes of waveforms.

## Inductive Biases and Assumptions Embedded
The model architecture assumes that `Iterated integrals of paths compactly encode geometric shapes of waveforms.`. By explicitly structuring the network to reflect this bias, we restrict the hypothesis space to physiologically plausible functions.

## Rejected Complexity
We reject over-parameterized black-box approaches in favor of a targeted inductive bias that can be validated via specific ablation variants.
