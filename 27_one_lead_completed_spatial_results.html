5.1 Experiment identity and architectural taxonomy

This completed study investigated ten distinct architectural and spatial conditioning strategies crossed with three factorial masks (1000000, 1010010, 1110000) and two observed leads (Lead I and Lead II), generating 60 completed training runs.

The ten architectural variants represent four distinct conceptual paradigms:

  1. Unconditioned Baselines: a0 (Standard 1-lead UNet baseline without lead coordinates).
  2. Coordinate & Angular Conditioning:
    • b1_panorama: Cylindrical panorama coordinate projection.
    • c1_panorama_hybrid: Hybrid coordinate embedding and feature concatenation.
    • d1_panorama_relative: Relative pairwise inter-lead angular displacement.
    • t000_exact_theta: 2D trigonometric angle encoding (\cos \theta, \sin \theta).
    • t111_exact_theta: Full 3D trigonometric orientation vectors.
  3. Advanced Modulation:
    • e1_panorama_film: True spherical/cylindrical geometry with Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM).
    • pa1_panorama_author: Author baseline spatial fusion formulation.
  4. Falsification & Capacity Controls:
    • cm1_capacity_matched: Parameter-matched control (identical parameter count to spatial variants but without anatomical coordinates).
    • pg1_permuted_geometry: Scrambled anatomical lead coordinates (tests whether physiological correctness matters).

5.6.1 Statistical Evaluation:

  1. E_1 vs Permuted Geometry (PG_1):
    • Mean improvement: +0.0063 Pearson (95% CI: [+0.0043, +0.0090]).
    • Paired t-test: t = 4.70, p = 0.0054; Wilcoxon signed-rank: W = 21, p = 0.0313.
    • Effect size: Cohen’s d = 1.92, Hedges’ g = 1.61.
    • Win Rate: 6/6 (100%). True anatomical coordinates consistently outperform scrambled coordinates.
  2. E_1 vs Capacity-Matched Control (CM_1):
    • Mean improvement: +0.0051 Pearson (95% CI: [+0.0031, +0.0076]).
    • Paired t-test: t = 4.88, p = 0.0045; Wilcoxon: p = 0.0313.
    • Win Rate: 6/6 (100%). Gains are not merely an artifact of extra parameter capacity.
  3. E_1 vs Baseline A_0:
    • Mean improvement: +0.0039 Pearson (95% CI: [-0.0016, +0.0108]).
    • Paired t-test: p = 0.283, Win Rate: 4/6. The overall gain against A_0 is driven predominantly by Mask 1000000 on Lead I (+0.0214), while other cells are near-zero ties.

## Experiment identity and architectural taxonomy

This completed study investigated ten distinct architectural and spatial conditioning strategies crossed with three factorial masks (`1000000`, `1010010`, `1110000`) and two observed leads (Lead I and Lead II), generating **60 completed training runs**.

The ten architectural variants represent four distinct conceptual paradigms:

1. **Unconditioned Baselines**: `a0` (Standard 1-lead UNet baseline without lead coordinates).
2. **Coordinate & Angular Conditioning**:
   - `b1_panorama`: Cylindrical panorama coordinate projection.
   - `c1_panorama_hybrid`: Hybrid coordinate embedding and feature concatenation.
   - `d1_panorama_relative`: Relative pairwise inter-lead angular displacement.
   - `t000_exact_theta`: 2D trigonometric angle encoding ($\cos \theta, \sin \theta$).
   - `t111_exact_theta`: Full 3D trigonometric orientation vectors.
3. **Advanced Modulation**:
   - `e1_panorama_film`: True spherical/cylindrical geometry with Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM).
   - `pa1_panorama_author`: Author baseline spatial fusion formulation.
4. **Falsification & Capacity Controls**:
   - `cm1_capacity_matched`: Parameter-matched control (identical parameter count to spatial variants but without anatomical coordinates).
   - `pg1_permuted_geometry`: Scrambled anatomical lead coordinates (tests whether physiological correctness matters).

| Factorial mask dominates variance | **Supported** | 3-way ANOVA demonstrates mask explains **54.7% of total variance** ($F = 306.9, p < 10^{-13}$). |
| Lead II contains superior frontal information | **Supported** | Explains **35.2% of variance**; systematic +0.05–0.12 Pearson across all variants. |
| Spatial conditioning improves clinical delineation | **Requires external evidence** | Validation logs record signal Pearson only; clinical endpoints evaluated in Chapter 26. |

## Interpretation limits

- All 60 completed runs were trained with seed 42; between-seed variance is not characterized here.
- Metric values are log-extracted validation screening metrics rather than held-out per-patient test sets.
- Transport to external datasets (RDB) is evaluated in the next chapter, where critical sign reversals occur.
