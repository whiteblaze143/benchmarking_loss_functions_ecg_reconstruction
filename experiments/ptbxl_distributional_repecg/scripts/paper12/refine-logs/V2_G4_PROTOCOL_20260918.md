# Paper 12 V2 G4 Diagnostic-Retention Protocol

**Frozen before fold 8 representation or label probing.** V1 remains failed. Corrected V2-G3 uses bijective, different-patient donor mappings; label-matched wrong history remains G3-only and is forbidden as a diagnostic representation.

## Splits and frozen upstream objects

- Folds 1–6: density fit, representation-specific label-free probe scaling and probe fit.
- Fold 7: one shared linear-probe configuration selected using phase-standardized state `Z` only.
- Fold 8: one-shot pseudo-test after every choice is frozen.

The 1,024-landmark map, canonical phase-coordinate standardizer and origin-0 density model are identical across representations and frozen before probes. Phase 0 is excluded, yielding 15,360 probe features.

## Representations

Primary hierarchy: raw Phase-KME state `X`, phase-standardized state `Z`, conditional mean residual `r`, and conditional standardized innovation `epsilon`. Secondary decomposition: predicted mean, predicted log-scale and their concatenation. A deterministic label-independent wrong-patient history may be reported secondarily.

Each representation receives its own label-free folds-1–6 probe mean and standard deviation with denominator floor `1e-6`. This scaling is probe conditioning only and does not redefine innovations.

## Probes

Primary is a linear five-label probe. On `Z`, train every learning-rate/weight-decay combination in `{1e-4,3e-4,1e-3}` by `{1e-5,1e-4,1e-3}` for 50 epochs with tuning seeds 52–54. Select one configuration and one epoch by the highest mean fold-7 macro AUROC across those seeds, with ties resolved by lower learning rate, lower weight decay and earlier epoch. Apply that single configuration and epoch to all four representations for ten paired confirmatory seeds 42–51. Batch size is 256. Seed and epoch determine one minibatch permutation reused identically across representations. There is no representation-specific early stopping. The existing 128-unit MLP is secondary and cannot alter the primary claim.

Every probe uses `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with one fold-1–6 prevalence-derived positive weight per class, `(1-prevalence)/prevalence`, capped at 10. The same five weights are frozen across representations and seeds.

## Endpoints and claims

Macro AUROC is primary. Macro AUPRC, classwise AUROC/AUPRC, Brier score and ECE are supplemental. Aggregation and 2,000-replicate inference are patient-clustered and use identical bootstrap patient draws across representations and seeds. Within each draw, all ECGs from sampled patients are retained with record weight equal to patient multiplicity divided by that patient's record count. Compute AUROC separately for every seed, average the ten AUROCs, and only then form representation contrasts and retention. Report seed-to-seed SD separately.

Report `AUC(Z)-AUC(X)`, `AUC(r)-AUC(Z)`, `AUC(epsilon)-AUC(r)` and chance-corrected retention `(AUC(epsilon)-0.5)/(AUC(Z)-0.5)`. Classwise retention ratios are reported only when `AUC(Z)>=0.55`; otherwise use absolute differences.

The primary G4 claim is weak retention: the lower endpoint of a two-sided 95% patient-bootstrap interval for innovation macro AUROC exceeds 0.5. Substantial retention is inferentially evaluated only if the lower two-sided 95% bound for `AUC(Z)` exceeds 0.55; it then requires the lower bound of chance-corrected retention to be at least 0.50. Otherwise the ratio is omitted. Near-preservation is a separate claim requiring the lower two-sided 95% bound of `AUC(epsilon)-AUC(Z)` to exceed `-0.02`; margins `-0.01` and `-0.005` are stricter descriptive sensitivities. Innovation superiority to state is not required.

Before fold-8 labels are unlocked, tests must assert 15,360 features for every primary representation, exclusion of phase 0, folds-1–6-only probe scalers, identical upstream hashes, a frozen density path, a shared configuration/epoch and minibatch order, identical bootstrap patient draws, and absence of label-matched wrong history from the G4 representation registry.

Bootstrap draws lacking positive or negative effective weight for any class are discarded and deterministically redrawn; the redraw count is reported and undefined AUROC is never replaced by 0.5. Within each valid draw compute classwise weighted AUROC, average the five classes, average the ten seeds, then form paired contrasts and retention. ECE is descriptive and uses 15 fixed equal-width probability bins.

Fold-7 selection writes an immutable `probe_config.json` containing LR, weight decay, epoch, batch size, positive weights, tuning seeds, metric and protocol hash. Confirmatory mode loads this artifact and is mechanically forbidden from running hyperparameter search. The single fold-8 invocation produces all representations, seeds, predictions, shared bootstrap draws, endpoints and claim decisions atomically.
