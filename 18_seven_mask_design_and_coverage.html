12.1 Why this is a new experiment, not an enlarged four-mask table

The legacy benchmark is a completed 3\times2^4=48 architecture-by-loss grid. The newer mask is a mixed-level design: fixed MSE, five binary additions, and one five-level MMD selector. It therefore contains 2^5\times5=160 conditions per architecture and seed. The last character is categorical; the study does not estimate seven binary effects.

Position Field Levels Scientific question
1 MSE fixed at 1 common reconstruction anchor
2 correlation 0/1 does scale-free waveform agreement help?
3 derivative 0/1 does local slope matching preserve transitions?
4 Kors VCG 0/1 does spatial-vector consistency help?
5 energy distance 0/1 does distributional matching help?
6 lead consistency 0/1 do electrophysiologic lead identities help?
7 MMD kernel 0–4 which distributional geometry, if any, is useful?

12.2 Live checkpoint reconciliation

The table is recomputed from the checkpoint catalog on every render. A registered checkpoint is not automatically a test-set result or a clinical evaluation.

At the current snapshot, U-Net is complete, MSVAE is missing five cells, and ECG-AIM is missing 37. Consequently, a full three-architecture factorial comparison is not yet estimable. Partial ECG-AIM rows are operational evidence, not a substitute for the missing cells.

Storage verification is not evaluation compatibility. The current compatibility audit accepts the 310 U-Net catalog rows but rejects the 155 MSVAE and 123 ECG-AIM archived rows under the present content-pinned source/state-schema/data contract. Those checkpoints remain recoverable and remotely verified, but a cross-architecture effect estimate is blocked until a generation-matched subset is established or the incompatible families are retrained.

12.3 Provenance gates

Every admissible comparison must match observed leads (I, II, V2), PTB-XL patient split, 500 Hz/no-normalization preprocessing, seed, source generation, checkpoint selector, and evaluation version. Mask strings alone are not unique experimental identities. Historical four-mask results and one-lead results are kept in separate chapters.

12.4 Claims this chapter permits

  • Exact statements about which checkpoints are registered and verified.
  • Exact statements about design structure and missing cells.
  • No claims about test performance from checkpoint counts.
  • No architecture-wide clinical claims until clinical evaluation coverage is reconciled separately.

## Why this is a new experiment, not an enlarged four-mask table

The legacy benchmark is a completed $3\times2^4=48$ architecture-by-loss grid.
The newer mask is a **mixed-level** design: fixed MSE, five binary additions,
and one five-level MMD selector. It therefore contains $2^5\times5=160$
conditions per architecture and seed. The last character is categorical; the
study does not estimate seven binary effects.

| Position | Field | Levels | Scientific question |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | MSE | fixed at 1 | common reconstruction anchor |
| 2 | correlation | 0/1 | does scale-free waveform agreement help? |
| 3 | derivative | 0/1 | does local slope matching preserve transitions? |
| 4 | Kors VCG | 0/1 | does spatial-vector consistency help? |
| 5 | energy distance | 0/1 | does distributional matching help? |
| 6 | lead consistency | 0/1 | do electrophysiologic lead identities help? |
| 7 | MMD kernel | 0–4 | which distributional geometry, if any, is useful? |
