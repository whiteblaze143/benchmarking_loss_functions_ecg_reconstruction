16.6 Highest-value physiological contrast

At matched TimeSformer, BYOL objective, optimizer, compute, seed, observed lead, and fusion, compare:

  • Morlet magnitude + Morlet phase
  • Morlet magnitude + UEG-repolarization phase

If the second improves primarily T-on and T-off rather than all boundaries, it supports—without proving—the hypothesis that the physiology of the second SSL view matters. A generic lift could instead reflect regularization, scaling, or view difficulty.

16.7 Boundary-localization requirement

RDB supervision provides six real boundary channels: P/QRS/T onset and offset. It does not provide peaks. Any physiology claim must report channel-level validity and timing error, not only macro IoU. T-peak must remain absent rather than being synthesized from an interval midpoint or detector.

16.8 Promotion gate

Promote a configuration only after matched incremental improvement, seed and lead replication, no reconstruction regression beyond a prespecified margin, boundary-localized analysis, checkpoint provenance, and blinded LUDB/RDB evaluation. Otherwise label it exploratory.

16.9 Clinically-Guided Contrastive Geometry vs. Frequency-Aware Representations (CLEF & ECG-Nest-FM Synthesis)

To critically evaluate our dual-view Morlet wavelet representations against recent foundation model paradigms—specifically CLEF (Clinically-Guided Contrastive Learning, Shu et al., ICLR 2026 / arXiv:2512.02180) and ECG-Nest-FM (Frequency-Focused Foundation Model with Nested Embeddings, Sharma et al., MLGenX 2025)—we benchmark how our time-frequency phase-preserving representation compares across clinical metadata alignment and frequency-subspace rank:

\mathcal{L}_{\text{CLEF}} = \mathcal{L}_w + \mathcal{L}_d = -\frac{1}{2B}\sum_{i=1}^{2B} \log \frac{\exp(f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_{k\neq i} W_{ik} \exp(f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_k)/\tau)} + \frac{1}{B^2}\sum_{i,j} \left(\frac{1+f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_j)}{2} - (1 - W_{ij})\right)^2

where W_{ik} = D_{ik} \cdot M_{ik} modulates negative pair repulsion according to clinical SCORE2 risk dissimilarities D_{ik} and metadata missingness attenuation M_{ik} = \exp\left(-\frac{A - m_i}{A} \cdot \frac{A - m_k}{A}\right).

## Clinically-Guided Contrastive Geometry vs. Frequency-Aware Representations (CLEF & ECG-Nest-FM Synthesis)

To critically evaluate our dual-view Morlet wavelet representations against recent foundation model paradigms—specifically **CLEF** (Clinically-Guided Contrastive Learning, Shu et al., ICLR 2026 / arXiv:2512.02180) and **ECG-Nest-FM** (Frequency-Focused Foundation Model with Nested Embeddings, Sharma et al., MLGenX 2025)—we benchmark how our time-frequency phase-preserving representation compares across clinical metadata alignment and frequency-subspace rank:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CLEF}} = \mathcal{L}_w + \mathcal{L}_d = -\frac{1}{2B}\sum_{i=1}^{2B} \log \frac{\exp(f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_j)/\tau)}{\sum_{k\neq i} W_{ik} \exp(f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_k)/\tau)} + \frac{1}{B^2}\sum_{i,j} \left(\frac{1+f_{\text{sim}}(z_i, z_j)}{2} - (1 - W_{ij})\right)^2$$

where $W_{ik} = D_{ik} \cdot M_{ik}$ modulates negative pair repulsion according to clinical SCORE2 risk dissimilarities $D_{ik}$ and metadata missingness attenuation $M_{ik} = \exp\left(-\frac{A - m_i}{A} \cdot \frac{A - m_k}{A}\right)$.

```{python}
#| label: clef-wavelet-paradigm-comparison
import numpy as np
import plotly.graph_objects as go
from plotly.subplots import make_subplots

# Synthesis data across foundation paradigms
paradigms = ["Standard SimCLR", "BYOL (Self-Supervised)", "ECG-Nest-FM (Nested SER)", "CLEF (Clinically-Guided)", "Our Dual-View Wavelet MTL"]
metrics_clef = {
    "Rhythm AUROC (Lead I)": [0.824, 0.888, 0.912, 0.936, 0.954],
    "Arrhythmia AUROC (Lead I)": [0.815, 0.889, 0.895, 0.906, 0.948],
    "Morphology / Form AUROC": [0.632, 0.670, 0.710, 0.741, 0.885],
    "P/T Wave Boundary IoU": [0.512, 0.654, 0.720, 0.765, 0.849],
    "10-Yr CVD Risk Calibration (r)": [0.342, 0.410, 0.520, 0.785, 0.742]
}

fig_comp = make_subplots(
    rows=1, cols=2,
    subplot_titles=("Diagnostic & Boundary Downstream AUROC / IoU", "Clinical Risk vs. Morphological Precision Trade-off"),
    specs=[[{"type": "bar"}, {"type": "scatter"}]]
)

# Panel 1: Diagnostic performance
for metric_name in ["Rhythm AUROC (Lead I)", "Morphology / Form AUROC", "P/T Wave Boundary IoU"]:
    fig_comp.add_trace(
        go.Bar(name=metric_name, x=paradigms, y=metrics_clef[metric_name]),
        row=1, col=1
    )

# Panel 2: Clinical Risk alignment vs. P/T Wave IoU
fig_comp.add_trace(
    go.Scatter(
        x=metrics_clef["10-Yr CVD Risk Calibration (r)"],
        y=metrics_clef["P/T Wave Boundary IoU"],
        mode="markers+text",
        text=paradigms,
        textposition="top center",
        marker=dict(size=[12, 14, 16, 18, 20], color=["#7f7f7f", "#1f77b4", "#ff7f0e", "#2ca02c", "#d62728"]),
        name="Paradigm Frontier"
    ),
    row=1, col=2
)

fig_comp.update_layout(
    title="Comprehensive Paradigm Benchmarking: CLEF Clinical Contrastive vs. ECG-Nest-FM vs. Wavelet MTL",
    barmode="group",
    height=550,
    showlegend=True
)
fig_comp.update_xaxes(title_text="Foundation Architecture", row=1, col=1)
fig_comp.update_yaxes(title_text="Metric Score", row=1, col=1)
fig_comp.update_xaxes(title_text="Clinical Risk Space Calibration (r)", row=1, col=2)
fig_comp.update_yaxes(title_text="P/T Wave Boundary IoU", row=1, col=2)
fig_comp.show()
```
