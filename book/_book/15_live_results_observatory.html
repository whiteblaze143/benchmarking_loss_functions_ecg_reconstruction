1.4 Visualization contract

The live chapters deliberately distinguish three objects:

  1. Raw metric views show observed values and remain the source of rankings.
  2. Matched deltas compare a configuration against its registered baseline only when seed and observed lead agree.
  3. Performance UMAP embeds standardized outcome vectors. Proximity means similar measured performance profiles; it does not establish similar encoder representations, physiology, or causal mechanisms.
