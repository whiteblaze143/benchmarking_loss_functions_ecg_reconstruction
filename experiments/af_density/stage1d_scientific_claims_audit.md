## 1. Executive Summary
This document provides a systematic, item-by-item audit of the claims made across the Stage 1, Stage 1B, and Stage 1C reports, cross-checked directly against the raw numerical evidence produced in Stage 1D.

Every previous scientific claim is classified under one of four audit dispositions:
1. **CONFIRMED:** Supported by authentic data, correct methodology, and reproducible artifacts.
2. **CORRECTED:** Partially valid observation, but previously attributed to the wrong mechanism or confounded by implementation artifacts.
3. **REFUTED:** Directly contradicted by empirical evidence or invalidated by methodological flaws.
4. **WITHDRAWN:** Overstated clinical claim unsupported by prospective evidence.

## 3. Methodological Breakdown of Calibration Inversion (P3)
A critical question raised in Stage 1C was why pooled calibrated AUROCs fell below 0.30 for several models despite Platt calibration. Stage 1D decomposition proved two distinct mathematical mechanisms:

1. **Weak Discriminability + Intercept Variance (Simpson's Paradox):**
   For pure morphology models (`A001`, `A003`, `A004`, `D032-v1`), within-patient AUROC was strictly preserved (e.g. `A001` raw = 0.5289, calibrated = 0.5289). However, because morphology discriminability is nearly flat ($\beta_1 \approx 0.05$), patient-to-patient differences in fold intercepts ($\beta_0 \in [-1.96, -1.45]$) shifted Patient A's probabilities relative to Patient B's, completely scrambling the cross-patient ranking when pooled.
2. **Negative Calibration Slopes ($\beta_1 < 0$):**
   In degenerate models (`A002`, `F053`, `I082-v1`), logistic regression slopes were negative across all folds, literally inverting the classifier polarity.

## 4. Final Scientific Posture
The scientific narrative must be honestly reframed:
- **Primary Finding:** Rhythm dynamics (R-R interval irregularity) represent the overwhelming driver of short-window AF detection ($>95\%$ of discriminative power). Non-neural GBDT provides a nearly unbeatable baseline (AUROC $>0.96$).
- **Morphology Reality:** Unsegmented Phase-KME continuous set operators do not provide meaningful clinical discrimination on this benchmark.
- **Path Forward:** Future research must focus on (1) Chronological beat-sequence modeling (Mamba / SSM / bidirectional RNNs over R-R intervals), (2) Symmetric lead-contrastive pretraining to prevent channel dropout collapse, and (3) Genuine segmented P-QRS-T beat morphology aligned to R-peaks.
