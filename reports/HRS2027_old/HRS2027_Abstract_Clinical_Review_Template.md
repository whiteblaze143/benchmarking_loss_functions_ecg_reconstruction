Background:
Cumulative atrial fibrillation (AF) burden omits how episodes cluster. Charitos AF density (0=scattered to 1=one block) measures clustering, yet its stability near continuous AF and sensitivity to short breaks are unclear.

Objective:
To determine how high-burden scaling, binary segment calls, R-R irregularity detection, and blinded ECG adjudication of short breaks alter AF density and continuity.

Methods:
