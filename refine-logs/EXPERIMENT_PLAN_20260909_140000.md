## 1. Executive Summary & Investigation Objective

In earlier factorial rounds, biophysical constraints (Kors 3D Vectorcardiographic loop loss) and distribution alignment metrics (Maximum Mean Discrepancy with IMQ, Laplacian, and temporal K-means kernels) suffered from **destructive gradient interference** when trained alongside point-wise MSE and Pearson correlation under static loss weights.

With Kendall homoscedastic uncertainty weighting (`adaptive_composite`) empirically validated as a **+0.0054 SOTA breakthrough**, this 11-job suite investigates:
> *Does adaptive log-variance optimization eliminate multi-objective gradient conflict, allowing 3D dipolar geometry and multiscale manifold alignment to enhance 12-lead reconstruction accuracy beyond point-wise tracking alone?*

---

## 2. Shared LCT-MTL Architectural Backbone

All 11 candidate configurations run on the empirically validated **LCT-MTL Champion Lean Base**:
- **Temporal Tokenization**: `--patch-size 10` ($20\text{ ms}$ at $500\text{ Hz}$)
- **Transformer Geometry**: 4 Encoder Layers, 4 Decoder Layers, `--width 384`, `--heads 16`
- **Spatial Input**: Dedicated Lead I (no whole-lead dropout: `--artificial-mask-mode no_lead_dropout`)
- **Auxiliary Delineation**: Multi-task wave delineation with boundary penalty (`--seg-ce-weight 1.0`, `--boundary-weight 0.2`, `--dice-weight 0.0`)
- **Multi-Task Optimization**: Homoscedastic uncertainty weighting (`--reconstruction-loss-type adaptive_composite`)
- **Input Normalization**: Per-sample Z-score standardization (`--zscore-norm`)
- **Safety Gate**: Epoch 10 early stopping threshold at $r \ge 0.7471$

## 4. Evaluation Protocol & Decision Gates

- **Split & Sample Size**: $N = 2,183$ PTB-XL held-out validation records (`split=val`).
- **Bootstrap Protocol**: 5,000 paired bootstrap resamples computing mean $\Delta r$, 95% two-sided confidence intervals, and one-sided $p_{\text{inferior}}$ values.
- **Decision Gates**:
  - **Gate 1 (Non-Inferiority)**: Missing-11 Pearson correlation must satisfy $p_{\text{inferior}} < 0.05$ against the Clean Lean Anchor ($r \ge 0.7552$).
  - **Gate 2 (Worst-Tail Preservation)**: 5th-percentile correlation must maintain $P_{05} \ge 0.4100$.
  - **Gate 3 (Biophysical Synergy Check)**: Any configuration that achieves $r > 0.7635$ and $P_{05} > 0.4208$ confirms biophysical synergy between spatial dipole constraints and fine temporal tokenization.
