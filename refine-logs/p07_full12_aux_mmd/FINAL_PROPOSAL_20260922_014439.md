# Research Proposal: Full-12-Lead Auxiliary Reconstruction for SetOperator

The method reuses the continuous SetOperator encoder and trains it on PTB-XL folds 1–7 with real observed lead subsets. For each batch it computes a sparse-view latent `h_sub` and a full-eight-independent-lead latent `h_full` for the same records. The primary objective is

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{BCE}}(\hat y_{\mathrm{sub}},y)
+ \widehat{\mathrm{MMD}}^2_{\mathrm{IMQ}}(\operatorname{LN}(h_{\mathrm{sub}}),\operatorname{LN}(h_{\mathrm{full}});c^2=1),
\]

where LayerNorm has no affine parameters. A training-only decoder predicts the real filtered, training-standardized 12-lead 5,000-sample ECG and adds `0.1 × MSE`. The target builder admits only ID-aligned PTB-XL folds 1–7/8 records and fits its 12-lead scaler on folds 1–7. Fold 8 selects only by diagnostic macro-AUROC. The deployed checkpoint contains the encoder state and is evaluated in the existing frozen task-native protocol. No test waveform, external cohort, fabricated target, or decoder inference is used.

The primary claim is conditional: this is a controlled auxiliary-objective variant, not evidence that full-ECG reconstruction improves clinical transfer until its registered comparison completes.
