# Paper 12 Location-Scale V2 Protocol

**Frozen before V2 execution.** V1 G3 remains an immutable failure. V2 tests train-only phase-coordinate standardization as a new scientific protocol.

## Representation

Using folds 1–6 only, fit a mean and population standard deviation for each canonical phase-coordinate pair of the frozen 1,024-landmark Phase-KME representation. Transform with `Z=(X-mean)/max(std,1e-6)`. Report the fraction floored overall and by phase. Fold 7 never updates these moments.

Phase-origin sensitivities rotate the already standardized canonical cells. They do not refit standardization. Downstream distances are not described as direct MMD geometry.

## Model

The conditional density model consumes `Z`. Its log-scale weights and bias initialize at zero, hence initial scale one. The V1 clamp `[-4,2]` is unchanged. All real-data fits retain width 64, AdamW learning rate `3e-4`, weight decay `1e-4`, batch 128, 1,000 updates and seed 42.

For each fresh seed 48/49/50, V2-G1 requires: dynamic-world innovation recovery MSE at most `0.02`; conditional NLL at least `0.05` below the train-fitted phase-only NLL; wrong-patient, label-matched wrong-history and record-specific phase-permutation NLL each at least `0.02` above correct-history NLL; and IID conditional improvement over phase-only no greater than `0.01`. Every condition must pass independently for every seed.

The real-data representation hierarchy is raw state, phase-standardized state, conditional mean residual and conditional standardized innovation. G4 will use an identical linear probe as primary for every representation and an identical MLP as secondary.

Calibration mean and variance remain descriptive. The hard real-data gates are the history/baseline/destroyer comparisons and the location/scale dependence directions at origins 0, 4, 8 and 12.
