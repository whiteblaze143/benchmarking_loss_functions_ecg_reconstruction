24 Engineering Integrity, Storage, and Reproducibility Atlas

This chapter audits the machinery that turns models and databases into reported evidence. It is deliberately operational: generation identity, storage accounting, SQLite health, output completeness, and build reproducibility. It does not manufacture scientific results when an optional analysis bundle is absent.

24.8 Interpretation boundary

Engineering integrity is necessary but not sufficient for scientific validity. A healthy database, complete queue, or matching hash cannot prove a clinical claim. Conversely, a scientifically sensible analysis is not releasable when generation identity, missingness, or execution state is ambiguous. This atlas makes those layers visible and treats unavailable optional artifacts as unavailable—not as zero, pass, or silently cached evidence.

## Interpretation boundary

Engineering integrity is necessary but not sufficient for scientific validity. A healthy database, complete queue, or matching hash cannot prove a clinical claim. Conversely, a scientifically sensible analysis is not releasable when generation identity, missingness, or execution state is ambiguous. This atlas makes those layers visible and treats unavailable optional artifacts as unavailable—not as zero, pass, or silently cached evidence.
